How to play

Goal
Fill your opponent's starting corner, shaded in their color.
Step
Move one piece a square up, down, left or right.
Jump
Or hop over any neighboring piece to the empty square beyond. Chain hops in one turn; nothing is captured.
No blocking
If your target corner is full and at least 5 of its pieces are yours, you win.
