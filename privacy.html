Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Summary

9 does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The game remembers your preferences – theme, move hints, jump setting, and the last game setup including any player names you typed – in your device's local storage so they are there next time. This data never leaves your device, and you can clear it at any time by clearing the app's or browser's storage.

Network

On the web, the game is loaded from haleli9.com. Like any website, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may process standard technical data such as your IP address to deliver the files. We do not receive or use this data. The iPhone app includes all of its files and works without a connection.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
