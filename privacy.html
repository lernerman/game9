Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary

9 has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share your data. To save your stats and let you play online with friends, the game keeps a small anonymous account for your device. You don't sign up, and we don't ask for your email, phone number or real name.

Your anonymous account

The first time you finish a game, open My stats, or play online, the game creates an anonymous account for your device. It stores:

  • A random account ID
  • The nickname you choose (optional)
  • Your game results: wins and losses, difficulty, playing time and number of moves
  • Online games you play: the board, the moves, both players' nicknames and the result

This data is used only to show your stats and run online games. The other player in an online game sees your nickname and your moves.

Where it is stored

Accounts and games are stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt). Like any online service, Supabase processes standard technical data such as IP addresses to deliver requests.

Deleting your data

Open My stats (the person icon at the top of the game) and tap "Delete my data". This permanently deletes your account, nickname and results from our server. Online games you played stay on the server without your account or nickname attached. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

What stays on your device

The game remembers your preferences – theme, move hints, jump setting, board orientation, and the last game setup including any names you typed – in your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you play online or save stats as described above.

Network

On the web, the game is loaded from haleli9.com, hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process standard technical data such as your IP address to deliver the files. Playing against the computer or a friend on the same device works without a connection.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We don't ask for personal information. Nicknames are visible to the other player in online games, so we recommend not using your full name.
